## What the estimate assumes

- **A module** is one crafter kept busy: miners on ore, furnaces when it needs plates, brick or glass, and the crafter, joined by belts and hand-offs, with the output carried away. Powered crafters (Foundry, Refinery) get one coal generator with its own coal miner. Feeders are sized 15% over what the crafter uses, so the crafter sets the pace.
- **Crafted ingredients** (gear, wire, steel, processor and so on) arrive by belt from the lines built in earlier tiers. The build allowance is the time to extend those lines.
- **Modules per tier:** 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 5, 6 (tiers 1–12). A tier's modules share its goals, so the minutes for each goal item add up.
- **Build allowance:** 6, 7, 8, 9, 12, 12, 14, 14, 16, 16, 18, 18 minutes (tiers 1–12).
- **Haul:** on tiers 5, 7, 9 and 11, the new ore's ring starts 28 / 48 / 66 / 84 tiles out; the first 10 tiles are free and the rest is travel time at that tier's belt speed. It is under a minute, because belts are fast; building the long line is part of the build allowance.
- **Research:** each tier uses only the upgrades bought in earlier tiers (the right-hand column above). Hot Furnaces and Quick Assembly speed up crafters, so they shorten "Making goals". Faster Miners and Deep Drills cut how many miners a module needs, and Faster Belts shortens the haul; none of them speeds up a crafter, so they mostly show up as less building, which the fixed build allowance does not count.
- **Crafter timing** is the sim's own: progress is zeroed when a craft finishes, so with speed upgrades a crafter runs slightly slower than its recipe time suggests. The measurement includes this.
- Not modelled: learning the controls, mistakes, rebuilding, idle time away from the game.

## If real play feels off

- **A tier drags or flies by:** change that tier's goals in `TIERS` in `src/config.js`. Making goals scales directly with them: double the goals, double the "Making goals" minutes.
- **Research feels slow or trivial:** change its `cost` in `RESEARCH`. Keep the cheapest node at each tier under 5 minutes of that tier's production.
- **One item is the bottleneck everywhere:** change its recipe `sec` in `RECIPES`. This moves every tier that uses it, so re-run this report.
- **Everything is uniformly too long or short:** players building more modules than assumed finish faster. Scale all goals together rather than touching recipe times.
- Re-run with `node tools/balance.mjs`; `npm test` fails if the estimate leaves 5–8 hours, tiers 1–3 leave 10–15 minutes, tiers 9–12 leave 40–50 minutes, or any tier's cheapest research passes 5 minutes.
