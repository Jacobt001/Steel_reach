# Steelreach balance report

Generated by `node tools/balance.mjs`. Do not edit by hand: change `src/config.js` and re-run.

**The model says a typical, well-practised player finishes tier 12 in about 354 minutes (5 h 54 min).** That is a best-case-ish model, not a forecast: real play will most likely run longer (see the last section). The target is 5–8 hours.

## What a "module" is

A module is one item's **whole chain from the ground up**: the crafter that makes the item, every crafter, furnace and miner upstream of it, and a coal generator (with its own coal miner) wherever a Foundry or Refinery needs power. Each stage is sized in whole machines so the final crafter never waits. Belts are not counted.

Example: a motor needs steel, gears and wire, which need iron plate and copper plate, which need furnaces and miners. The module is all of that, not just the motor fabricator.

Machine speeds come from the real simulation: each crafter is run for 6,000 ticks with the research the player owns at that tier. The chain sizes come from walking each recipe's ingredients back to ore.

### The building budget

A phone player will not place a 40-building chain for every item. The model assumes they place at most this many buildings per module at each tier: **10, 10, 10, 12, 12, 14, 14, 16, 16, 18, 18, 20** (tiers 1–12). If a chain is bigger, the module runs at budget ÷ chain size of full speed, as if the player stopped short of a fully fed line.

Why these numbers: they start at 10 (a few furnaces, miners and an assembler) and grow to 20 as the player gets quicker at laying lines and gets tunnels, bridges and poles. They are a planning assumption, not a measurement. If real players build less, the late tiers take longer (see levers below).

## What the estimate leaves out

- **Haul:** on tiers 5, 7, 9 and 11 the new ore's ring starts 28 / 48 / 66 / 84 tiles out. The first 10 tiles are free and the rest is travel time at that tier's belt speed. It comes to under a minute; building the long line is part of the build allowance.
- **Research effects:** Hot Furnaces and Quick Assembly speed up crafters and so shorten "Making goals". Faster Miners and Deep Drills mainly mean fewer miners in a chain, which the fixed building budget does not credit.
- **Crafter timing** is the sim's own: progress is zeroed when a craft finishes, so with speed upgrades a crafter runs slightly slower than its recipe time suggests.
- Not modelled: learning the controls, mistakes, rebuilding, jams, idle time away from the game, and longer hauls than the straight-line distance.

## Real play will likely run longer

This is a model of a player who knows what to build and places it in one go. A first-time player will spend more time learning, fixing mistakes and being away, so expect the real total to land above the number at the top. If it runs too long, these are the levers, biggest first:
- **Lower the late goals** in `TIERS` in `src/config.js`. "Making goals" scales directly: halve a goal, halve its minutes.
- **Raise the building budget** (what a player can fit per module) by shrinking the chains: the biggest are robot arm, turbine and fusion core. Cheaper recipe inputs make smaller chains.
- **Research feels slow or trivial:** change its `cost` in `RESEARCH`. Keep the cheapest node at each tier near 3–4 minutes.
- **One item is the bottleneck everywhere:** change its recipe `sec`. That moves every tier that uses it, so re-run this report.
- Re-run with `node tools/balance.mjs`. `npm test` fails if the total leaves 5–8 hours, tiers 1–3 leave 10–15 minutes, tiers 9–12 leave 40–50 minutes, or a tier fails both research checks.
